About

I'm an engineering student at the Higher Institute of Computer Science (ISI) in Tunis, specialising in embedded systems and connected objects.

My work sits where hardware meets software: STM32 and ESP32 firmware, real-time operating systems, sensors, mobile and web apps, and machine learning models that run on small devices. I like turning a messy, real-world problem into something that works reliably.

I'm curious and rigorous, and I'm looking for opportunities in embedded systems, IoT, full-stack development and applied AI.
